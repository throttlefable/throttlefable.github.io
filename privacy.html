Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

This policy covers the Throttle Fable publishing tool (the "App"): a private tool that the owner of the Throttle Fable YouTube channel uses to upload the channel's own videos. The App is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data the App accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the App asks for these permissions through the YouTube API Services:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to upload Throttle Fable videos to the owner's channel.
  • View the YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to read the channel name and ID and the status of uploaded videos.

The App does not read, collect or store data about any other YouTube user, viewer or commenter.

How the data is used and stored

  • The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer, encrypted, and is used only to upload videos and check their status.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • The App's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube and Google

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google Account permissions page. Deleting the stored token from the owner's computer removes all data the App keeps.
